Market Rules

Each market is a parimutuel pool on the streamer's concurrent viewer count. You stake USDC on one of nine viewer brackets. When the market settles, everyone who picked the winning bracket splits the entire pool in proportion to their stake, minus a 2% fee. Odds shown are an estimate from the current pool and shift as more bets come in.

Settlement: after betting closes, an automated oracle samples the live viewer count once per minute over a sampling window. The market resolves to the bracket containing the trimmed mean of those samples (extreme high/low samples are discarded to resist manipulation). Winnings unlock after a short challenge window.

Void & refunds: if the stream goes offline before enough samples are collected, or no one bet the winning bracket, the market is declared Void and every staker can reclaim their full stake. Streamers cannot force-close or settle markets.

Per-wallet cap: each wallet can stake at most 50 USDC per market.
